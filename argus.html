ARGUS Trekking Safety System

A wearable and portal system for trekker safety and emergency response, built with a team of four at the Build Nepal Hackathon.

Build Nepal Hackathon Mid-Valley International College Aug 1–2, 2026

What It Does

ARGUS pairs a wrist-worn vitals monitor with a phone app and a web backend, so trekkers in areas with little to no signal can still get help if something goes wrong. A trekker's status and location sync when possible, and an authority-side portal lets rescue teams see who might be in trouble and respond faster.

Wristband

ESP32 microcontroller with a MAX30102 sensor for heart rate and blood oxygen, paired to a phone over Bluetooth.

Location & Alerts

Uses the phone's GPS for positioning and can push alerts out over WhatsApp when a trekker needs help.

Trekker & Authority Portals

A Next.js and Supabase backend behind two portals: one for trekkers, one for the authorities monitoring them.
