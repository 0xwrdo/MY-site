21V RC Car

Rebuilt from scratch on a BTS7960 driver and a FlySky FS-i6 radio, running off a 21V pack with roughly 2km of control range.

21V Pack ~2km Range Full / Half Power Modes

Technical Features

BTS7960 Motor Driver

A 43A H-bridge driver running both rear motors in parallel, with R_EN/L_EN wired straight to 5V so it's always enabled.

FlySky FS-i6 Radio

FS-i6 transmitter and FS-iA6 receiver for control, with a switch channel wired in for selectable power modes.

Split Power Rails

21V goes straight to the motor driver; a separate regulated rail powers the Arduino, receiver, and steering servo so they don't brown out under motor load.

Build Notes

Controller
Arduino Uno: reads throttle (CH2) and the power-mode switch (CH5) via pulseIn()
Steering
SG90 servo wired directly into the receiver's CH4: no Arduino in that signal path
Drive
2 DC motors in parallel through a single BTS7960, PWM-driven from the Arduino
Power
21V pack feeds the BTS7960 directly; a separate regulated rail feeds the Arduino, receiver, and servo
Failsafe
Motors cut out on signal loss instead of holding a stale throttle value
Ground
Battery, driver, Arduino, receiver, and servo all share a common ground
