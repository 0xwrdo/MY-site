Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and how to get it removed.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Overview

This is a personal portfolio site for Raunak Kushwaha. It is self-hosted on a Raspberry Pi and does not run any advertising, does not sell data, and does not share visitor data with third parties for marketing purposes.

What Gets Collected

Standard server logs are kept for security and debugging. These may include your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. If this site uses an analytics tool, it will be listed here along with what it tracks.

Contact Forms & Email

Contact links on this site go directly to email or messaging apps (for example, a mailto: link). Nothing you type is stored by this site itself, since there is no server-side contact form processing your message.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. If that changes, a cookie notice will be added here and a consent banner will be shown on your first visit.

Third-Party Services

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Icons are loaded from Font Awesome via a CDN.
  • The site is served through Cloudflare, which may log connection metadata as part of its network service.

These providers may collect basic connection data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service, under their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

You can request details on any data held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing raunakkushwaha077@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date above reflects the most recent update.
